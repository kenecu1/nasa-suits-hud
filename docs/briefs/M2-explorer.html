NASA SUITS HUD · milestone 2 · before building

M2 alerts, hands on

An interactive companion to docs/briefs/M2.md. Nothing here is the real code. Each tab is a small model of the design, so you can poke at it before a builder writes it. Ranges come from the real data/ranges.json. Fault behaviour, rates and NASA's action wording come from the TSS notes and NASA's TSS code at commit 7dfc42b. Where the brief has an open decision, the model follows its recommendation and says so.

Text sizes at panel distance

    
dmm is an angle: 1 dmm = 1 mm seen from 1 m. Size in mm = dmm × distance in m. The panel uses 1 px = 1 mm, so px = mm. M1's sizes were in px, which makes their dmm shrink as the panel moves away. Minimum: 24 dmm.
